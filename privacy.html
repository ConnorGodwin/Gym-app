Privacy Policy

Forge · Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: Forge has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics. Everything you log (workouts, food, weight, measurements and photos) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

What Forge stores, and where

Workouts, plans, food diary, targets, bodyweight, measurements, progress photos and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to any server run by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently. You can export a backup file from Settings, and it is up to you where you keep it.

Information that leaves your device

WhenWhat is sentTo whom
Searching for a food or looking up a barcodeYour search words or the barcode numberOpen Food Facts, a non-profit food database
Playing an exercise videoA request for the video fileThe website that hosts Forge (GitHub Pages) in the web version; in the store apps videos are bundled
Tapping “More demos on YouTube”The exercise name, in your browserYouTube (Google), under their own privacy policy

No personal details, health data or identifiers are attached to these requests by Forge.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, take progress photos. Images are processed and stored on your device.
  • Photos: only when you choose a progress photo from your library.
  • Notifications: only to alert you when a rest timer or countdown finishes. They are scheduled on your device.

Health information

Weight, body measurements, food intake and training records are health-related information. Forge uses them only to show your progress and to calculate suggestions inside the app. They are never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Children

Forge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Calorie targets, training plans and suggestions are general fitness information and are not medical advice. Check with a doctor or qualified professional before starting a new diet or exercise programme, especially if you have a medical condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
