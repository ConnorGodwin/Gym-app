# Changelog

The version number matches `VERSION` in `sw.js` and Settings → App in the app.

## v12
- Edit a saved workout (sets, exercises, date, time, duration), or log a past workout from scratch or from a plan day.
- Automated tests (unit and browser) that run on GitHub for every push.

## v11
- Body units: bodyweight in kg, lb or st & lb; height in cm or ft & in; measurements in cm or in. These are separate from the lifting unit.
- 24 new plan templates (34 in total), grouped by category: running distances, triathlon, swimming, rowing, team, racket and combat sports, climbing, golf, ski prep, and health and mobility.
- 62 new exercises: sport sessions, agility and plyometrics, combat rounds, climbing and balance work.

## v10
- Exercise demos from wger: 137 exercises with images, 26 with videos, and step-by-step instructions.
- Removed the one-off v7 upgrade code.

## v9
- Food search rebuilt on Open Food Facts' fast search, with nutrition per product; fixes "couldn't reach the food database".
- New bottom-sheet header with close and back buttons.

## v8
- Food diary, barcode scanning, recipes, meal plans, shopping list, targets and adaptive check-in, water.
- Body tracking (weight trend, measurements, photos), Progress tab (summary, streaks, heat-map, muscle map, records, achievements).
- Warm-ups, set types, RPE, plate calculator, supersets, weekly schedule, deloads, HYROX race mode.
- Data layer ready for future cloud sync.

## v7
- Update banner and Check for updates.

## v6 and earlier
- Accent colours, light/dark mode, mobile polish, workout tracking with progression suggestions, timers and history.
